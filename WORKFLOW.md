## 1. The approval pipeline

Every client-facing artifact moves through this pipeline, in order:

```
specialist drafts  →  team lead reviews  →  Shannon McNeil approves  →  client
```

- **Step 1 — Specialist drafts.** The responsible specialist (Audit & Research Analyst, Content Strategist, Paid Media Specialist, or Reporting Analyst) produces the artifact as an internal draft in the shared workspace.
- **Step 2 — Team lead reviews.** The team lead checks the draft for accuracy, consistency, source-verification, and store-by-store specificity before it goes up.
- **Step 3 — Shannon McNeil approves.** Shannon McNeil reviews and gives sign-off. Her approval is the gate — nothing proceeds without it.
- **Step 4 — Client.** Only Shannon McNeil sends approved material to the client.

## 2. Non-negotiables

- **No agent ever contacts the client.** All client communication goes through Shannon McNeil.
- **Every client-facing artifact is a draft until approved.** A draft that has not received Shannon McNeil's sign-off must never be presented as final or sent out.
- **Nothing invented.** Every claim carries a real, citable source or is marked "TO VERIFY — request from client via Shannon McNeil". Never fabricate facts, statistics, or benchmarks about Greenway Auto Group's dealerships.
- **Store-by-store, not group templates.** Every deliverable is built per rooftop. A finding that applies to one store is stated for that store, not generalized across all five. Each rooftop is its own market with its own GM, its own competitive set, and its own content voice; adoption is voluntary per GM (no top-down mandate). Never produce a single "group template" deliverable where five store-specific ones are required.
- **GM-ready output.** Deliverables hand to a dealership General Manager without rework: plain language, actionable, no agency jargon.
- **Train-the-trainer.** The long-term objective is moving social capability in-house at the store level. Every deliverable is written so a store employee could eventually run it without Bright Matter.

## 3. Status conventions

- **TO VERIFY — request from client via Shannon McNeil** — data the client must provide before real work can start (handles, access, ownership, goals, store identity).
- **Unknown** — default status for any channel whose active/inactive state has not been confirmed.
- **Draft** — internal, not approved, not client-facing.
- **Approved** — Shannon McNeil has signed off; only then may it leave the team.

## 4. Starting real audits

Real per-store audits cannot begin until Shannon McNeil provides the data listed in `CLIENT.md` §8 (per-store channel handles/URLs, active status, access, ownership, and engagement goals). Store identity (names, markets, GMs) is verified; until channel data arrives, all channel data in `stores/` and `channel-matrix.csv` remains at default TO VERIFY / Unknown values.
