## The core idea (why these five) — format decides, not topic

Revision 1's single wedge — "competitors are stale, so post current content and own the lane" — **did not survive** on the channels the pilot publishes to (CONFIRMED 2026-08-22):

- **KC (Store 05) is not empty.** **Bob Sight Ford** posts near-daily (8.3K FB followers) and writes sharper local-geography trim comparisons than our own rev-1 examples. **Rob Sight Ford** (156 followers) out-reaches Bob Sight ~**78× per follower** (9.4 vs 0.12 plays/follower) on **scrappy personality** content. The KC opening is **quality/watchability, not currency.**
- **Jacksonville (Store 02) is wide open by contrast.** **Kia on Atlantic** posts weekly promos into **near-zero engagement** (0 likes / 0 shares on 4 of 5 FB posts; static-graphic format) — Store 02's **depth + utility + local** wedge is *stronger* than rev-1 claimed.
- **What works at Greenway is structural, not topical.** Greenway Kia West's biggest posts are a **named recurring character** ("Zarya", 1.2M plays), **serialized campaigns** ("Help Zarya get to 15k", 22+ days), and **launch walkarounds** (2027 Seltos, 216.6K). Static images/carousels landed 0–15 likes; every Reel landed 342–13,055 plays. **Format decides, not topic.**
- Store 01's competitor gap is now **actionable via open-market discovery** (same method that confirmed Store 02's set).

## The format tier layer (NEW — every counted post carries a tier)

| Tier | What it is | Counted in mix? |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Hero** | Recurring named character · serialized campaign · **launch walkaround**. Where the ceiling lives. Target ≥1/rooftop/week in full mix. | ✅ Counted |
| **B — Workhorse** | Video, no character required: feature demos, service explainers, comparisons, offer-as-video, delivery-day. The reliable ~2,000-play IG baseline. | ✅ Counted |
| **C — Utility** | **Published but NOT counted:** per-VIN walkarounds, static offer/compliance graphics, hours/holiday notices, roundup photos, review-milestone graphics. Lives on YouTube/GBP/Facebook. | ❌ NOT counted |

**Two hard rules:** (1) every counted social post is Tier A or Tier B — Tier C never fills a mix slot; (2) an offer is published as **video with a person**, or it is not published as a social post.
