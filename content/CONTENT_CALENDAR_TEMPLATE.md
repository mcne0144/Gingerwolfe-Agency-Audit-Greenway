**Filling rules (per-store specificity — never group-template filler):**

**Approval rules (from `WORKFLOW.md`):**
- Pipeline is **always**: specialist drafts → team lead reviews → **Shannon McNeil approves** → posted (by Shannon, or by the store at her direction). No step is skipped, and no agent contacts the client.
- An item that comes back with **Edits needed** goes back to `Draft` and re-enters the pipeline — it is never posted in a rejected state.
- A **TO VERIFY** cell that is still unresolved on the post date means the item is held, not posted. The calendar row keeps its status and the item is re-slotted with Shannon's agreement.
- Monthly roll-up for the pilot overview (§2): count `Approved` vs `Posted` vs `Pending` and record in the month's row.
