## 0. What this playbook is

The handoff plan for the pilot's long-term objective: **a store employee eventually runs the store's social in-house, without Bright Matter.** It is the curriculum and operating rhythm that turns a store employee into the store's content operator over six months, while the approval chain stays intact the whole way (`GOVERNANCE_MODEL.md` §9).

The playbook is a framework with per-store application. One trainee per rooftop is **TO VERIFY — request from client via Shannon McNeil**. If a store has no trainee, the pilot simply does not hand off there — the store's content still runs through the agency pipeline. No store is pushed into training it did not choose.

Two rules govern everything in this playbook:
1. **Approval never transfers during the pilot.** Shannon McNeil approves every post until the Month 6 readout, and the store decides what happens after.
2. **No dependency on any individual store employee.** The pilot's content operation works whether a trainee joins, leaves, or never exists (`GOVERNANCE_MODEL.md` §7.3).

## 1. The 6-month progression

| Month | Stage | What the trainee does | Who reviews | Handoff marker |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Month 3 | **Observe** | Watches the pipeline: calendar, briefs, drafts, approvals, publishing. Sees guardrails applied to real content | None (trainee produces nothing) | Trainee can explain the approval chain and the five pillars in plain words |
| Month 4 | **Co-create** | Proposes topics, drafts captions and scripts alongside the Content Strategist, attends review feedback | Content Strategist edits; team lead reviews; Shannon approves | Trainee drafts at least 3 approved items in one month |
| Month 5 | **Own drafts under review** | Drafts routine store content start to finish; handles scheduling prep and response drafts | Content Strategist first review; team lead and Shannon unchanged | Trainee's drafts need only minor edits for 4 consecutive weeks |
| Month 6 | **Run with agency support** | Drafts and (at Shannon's direction) schedules; responds to comments and reviews with Shannon-approved language | Shannon approves; team lead spot-checks; weekly batch review replaces per-item review | Month 6 readiness check (§8): store can sustain cadence, tone, compliance, and reporting |

**Rules for the progression:**
1. Stages advance one at a time and only on the handoff marker. A trainee who is not ready stays in the current stage; that is a capacity finding, not a failure.
2. Sensitive items never land on the trainee's desk: reputation findings, salesperson-content issues, and compliance questions stay with the team and Shannon (`GOVERNANCE_MODEL.md` §8).
3. Stores 03 and 04 progress on separate tracks even with one GM. Their trainees are separate people (TO VERIFY) with separate calendars.
4. If the trainee leaves mid-pilot, the store either names a new trainee (restarting at the observe stage) or the store's handoff plan pauses. The pilot's content operation is unaffected — the agency pipeline still runs it.

## 2. Curriculum outline — what a store employee learns

Five modules, run one per stage month (observe month covers modules 1–2 at intro level; co-create month covers 3–4; own month covers 4–5 deeply; run month consolidates all).

### Module 1 — How the pipeline works
1. The approval chain: specialist drafts → team lead reviews → Shannon approves → published by Shannon via Sprout. Why no step is skipped.
2. The calendar (`CONTENT_CALENDAR_TEMPLATE.md`): content IDs, statuses, the approval tracker, the mix check.
3. The creative brief (`CREATIVE_BRIEF.md`): brief before copy, every time.

### Module 2 — Voice and guardrails
1. The five pillars and the store's mix percentages (`CONTENT_STRATEGY_FRAMEWORK.md` §3, §7).
2. Voice principles: honest first, specific beats vague, helpful beats pushy, human not corporate, local first (§4).
3. The guardrails: no invented facts, no fake urgency, OEM rules TO VERIFY per store, Reddit participates never promotes, GBP responses genuine within 2 business days (§5–6, §8).
4. The TO VERIFY discipline: what gets marked, who confirms it, what happens to an unresolved item (held, not posted).

### Module 3 — Drafting
1. Turning a calendar slot and brief into copy: hook, value, CTA, sign-off (one CTA per piece).
2. Photo and video sourcing: real, current, on-brand; what the store can supply (TO VERIFY assets); no stock filler.
3. Channel formats: photo posts, carousels, Reels, Stories, Shorts, long-form, GBP posts, Reddit participation.

### Module 4 — Scheduling and publishing (Sprout)
1. Sprout Social is operated by Shannon. The trainee learns what Shannon allows: building drafts, scheduling within approved items, the calendar view, and (at Shannon's grant) a limited scheduling role. Agents never touch Sprout (`OPERATING_RULES.md` §2).
2. How approval status maps to Sprout state: only `Approved` items are scheduled; `Draft`, `Lead review`, and `Edits needed` never are.
3. Native tools the store may use at Shannon's direction (e.g., Instagram Stories shot on a phone), and how they are logged in the calendar.

### Module 5 — Responding and reporting
1. Comment and DM response: who responds, response-time targets, escalation of anything sensitive to Shannon (never to the trainee's discretion alone).
2. Review responses on GBP: genuine, specific, within 2 business days; the store's review link; how to log each response in the calendar.
3. The monthly report cycle (`reporting/GM_KPI_FRAMEWORK.md` §4): what gets captured, when, and how the GM report is produced. The trainee learns to read the store's dashboard, not to invent numbers.

## 3. Weekly operating rhythm (per store, per week)

| Day | Activity | Who | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | Weekly content review: this week's calendar items, briefs, drafts | Content Strategist + trainee (observe+ stages) | Updated calendar statuses |
| Monday | Asset check: what photos/video the store must supply this week | Trainee asks store contact (via Shannon) | Asset list with TO VERIFY status |
| Tuesday–Wednesday | Drafting and editing for next week's slots | Content Strategist drafts; trainee co-drafts or owns per stage | Drafts at `Draft` status |
| Thursday | Team lead review of the batch | Team lead | `Lead review` → `Shannon review` or `Edits needed` |
| Friday | Shannon approval + Sprout scheduling for next week | Shannon McNeil | `Approved` items scheduled |
| Friday | Response sweep: comments, DMs, reviews, Q&A | Trainee drafts responses; Shannon approves; store responds at her direction | Response log updated |
| Monday (next) | Mix check prep and retro notes at month end | Trainee + Content Strategist | Feeds next month's calendar (`CONTENT_CALENDAR_TEMPLATE.md` §4) |

**Note:** the rhythm is a target, not a promise. Cadence is a Shannon-approved capacity decision per store (TO VERIFY — `CONTENT_STRATEGY_FRAMEWORK.md` §8). If a store cannot staff Monday asset checks, the rhythm adapts and the calendar absorbs it — consistency beats volume.

## 4. Checklists per role

### 4.1 Store trainee (weekly)
1. ☐ Read this week's calendar rows for my store; know each item's status.
2. ☐ Confirm assets the store owes (photos, video, vehicle details) and flag anything missing as TO VERIFY.
3. ☐ Draft assigned items per their briefs; mark nothing approved that is not.
4. ☐ Log every comment/DM/review I drafted a response for, with the response deadline.
5. ☐ Ask before acting when a rule is unclear; never guess an OEM or platform rule.
6. ☐ Keep the training log: what I practiced this week, what I got stuck on.

### 4.2 GM (one-on-one only, never group)
1. ☐ Confirm or decline the store's participation and trainee (TO VERIFY names).
2. ☐ Review the monthly GM report with Shannon, one-on-one.
3. ☐ Receive sensitive findings (reputation, salesperson content, staff dependency) one-on-one, framed as absence and opportunity (`OPERATING_RULES.md` §9–10).
4. ☐ Decide store-level items only: adoption, trainee, assets, capacity. Never approve individual posts during the pilot.
5. ☐ Tell the sales manager about the salesperson-content framework per `SALESPERSON_CONTENT_FRAMEWORK.md` §8, if adopted.

### 4.3 Content Strategist (throughout)
1. ☐ Produce briefs and drafts per the calendar; keep every TO VERIFY marker live.
2. ☐ Run the Monday review with the trainee at their stage; explain edits, not just make them.
3. ☐ Escalate sensitive items to the team lead before they reach Shannon.
4. ☐ Run the monthly mix check with the trainee (`CONTENT_CALENDAR_TEMPLATE.md` §4).

### 4.4 Shannon McNeil (every week)
1. ☐ Approve or return the week's batch with specific reasons (no silent drops — `GOVERNANCE_MODEL.md` §4).
2. ☐ Operate Sprout: schedule approved items; grant trainee Sprout access only at her discretion.
3. ☐ Route the store's asset requests and TO VERIFY list to the store contact.
4. ☐ Hold one-on-one GM conversations for anything sensitive.

### 4.5 Team lead
1. ☐ Review all drafts before Shannon; check accuracy, sourcing, and store specificity.
2. ☐ Track the handoff progression per store in the training log; flag stalls.
3. ☐ Own the escalation path (`GOVERNANCE_MODEL.md` §8) and the expansion log.

## 5. What stays with the agency vs what transfers in-house

| Function | During the pilot (Months 1–6) | After Month 6 (store's decision) |
|---|---|---|
| Content strategy (pillars, mix, cadence) | Agency (Content Strategist) | Transfers: the store's STRATEGY.md and playbooks are Greenway's property (`CLIENT.md` §4) |
| Calendar planning | Agency, with trainee co-planning | Transfers with the playbook and calendar templates |
| Drafting routine content | Agency with trainee (per stage) | Transfers to trainee; agency supports optionally |
| Copy review | Content Strategist → team lead | Store's internal review (GM or designee); the store decides |
| Final approval | **Shannon McNeil — never transfers during the pilot** | Store's decision: GM approval or a store operator |
| Publishing / scheduling | **Shannon only, via Sprout** | Store's operator via Sprout (licensing billed at cost, `CLIENT.md` §4) or another tool the store chooses |
| Comment/DM/review response | Shannon-approved language; store responds at her direction | Store's operator, with the response playbook |
| Reporting | Reporting Analyst produces GM report; Shannon approves | Store reads its dashboard; Bright Matter support optional |
| Audit and benchmarking | Agency (Audit & Research Analyst) | Playbook and benchmarks are Greenway's property; re-verification is the store's job |
| Salesperson-content framework | Shannon per GM, one-on-one | Store's adopted version stays with the store |
| Paid media | Out of scope (hard line, `OPERATING_RULES.md` §2) | Separate decision; never assumed |

**The transfer test:** a function transfers only when the store can run it to the same standard without the agency: correct cadence, correct tone, no unverified facts, approvals documented. The Month 6 readiness check (§8) applies that test function by function.

## 6. The training log

One internal record per store (future file: `content/governance/training/store-XX/TRAINING_LOG.md`, created when a trainee is named — TO VERIFY):

1. ☐ Trainee name and role (TO VERIFY — request from client via Shannon McNeil).
2. ☐ Start stage and date; stage advances with the handoff marker (§1).
3. ☐ Weekly entry: items drafted, items approved, edits received, questions asked.
4. ☐ Monthly entry: mix-check participation, response-sweep coverage, anything the trainee escalated.
5. ☐ Sensitive items the trainee was correctly kept off of (recorded as category only, no names).
6. ☐ Month 6 readiness check score (§8) and the GM's handoff decision.

The log is internal. Nothing about individual trainees is client-facing unless Shannon approves.

## 7. Failure modes and how the model absorbs them

1. **Trainee stops progressing.** The Content Strategist flags it at the weekly review; the team lead tells Shannon; the store's stage freezes and the calendar continues on the agency pipeline. No content gap.
2. **Store loses its trainee.** Pause the store's handoff; agency runs content; a new trainee restarts at observe. The playbook is a ladder, not a cliff.
3. **Store wants to jump ahead.** A GM asking for early publishing access is a governance question, answered by the model: approval stays with Shannon through Month 6, and the playbook says why (traceability, consistency, single voice). The GM's ask itself is logged as an expansion signal for the Month 6 conversation.
4. **Trainee drafts violate guardrails.** That is a training outcome, not a scandal: the draft is held (never posted), the edit is explained, and the Module 2 material is revisited. Repeat violations in one topic update the store's training plan.
5. **No trainee at any store.** The pilot still delivers content, calendars, and reporting for six months; the handoff simply does not occur. Train-the-trainer is the long-term objective, not a Month 6 obligation on any single rooftop.

## 8. Month 6 readiness check

Run per store in Month 6, one-on-one with the GM via Shannon, using evidence from the training log. Pass/fail is the store's call; the check just makes the evidence visible.

| Readiness question | Evidence from | Pass means |
|---|---|---|
| Can the store sustain cadence for 4 weeks without per-item agency edits? | Calendar, approval tracker, training log | Trainee drafts met the store's cadence at minor-edit quality |
| Does the store's operator know the five pillars, the guardrails, and the TO VERIFY discipline? | Module 2 assessment (conversation + a sample draft) | Explains each in plain words; spots an unverified claim in a sample |
| Does the store's operator know how to schedule and respond? | Module 4–5 practice records | Can schedule approved items and draft responses to the standard |
| Can the store produce the monthly report from its dashboard? | Module 5 records | Reads the dashboard, flags gaps, never invents numbers |
| Is the approval chain for post-pilot operations defined? | GM decision | The store names who approves and who publishes after Month 6 |
| Is the store's adopted salesperson framework in place? | `SALESPERSON_CONTENT_FRAMEWORK.md` §8 session record | Team session ran; one-page summary distributed |

**Outcomes:** ready (hand off per the store's decision), partially ready (hand off some functions per the transfer test in §5), or not ready (agency continues under the current or a new arrangement; the conversation is opportunity-framed, never a verdict on the trainee).

## 10. Relationship to the rest of the workspace

1. **Governance first.** `GOVERNANCE_MODEL.md` §9 is the approval-side view of this playbook; the playbook is the curriculum-side view. They never contradict: approval stays with Shannon throughout.
2. **The calendar is the classroom.** Every skill in the curriculum is practiced on the store's real calendar rows (`CONTENT_CALENDAR_TEMPLATE.md`), never on fake exercises.
3. **Reporting closes the loop.** The trainee learns to read the dashboard the Reporting Analyst maintains (`reporting/GM_KPI_FRAMEWORK.md`); the monthly GM report is the trainee's textbook.
4. **Salesperson content is separate.** A trainee is a content operator; salespeople on personal accounts are covered by `SALESPERSON_CONTENT_FRAMEWORK.md`. The two never merge into "everyone posts whatever."
