# CONTENT_MODELS.md — 5 Reusable Content Models for the Greenway Pilot

**Status:** INTERNAL RESEARCH DRAFT — not client-facing. These models feed `content/CONTENT_STRATEGY_FRAMEWORK.md` pillar definitions and the per-store strategy (`content/strategies/store-XX/STRATEGY.md`). Marked which need verification before the operator uses them in a client-facing calendar.
**Prepared by:** Bright Matter LLC agency operations team (Content Strategist)
**Basis:** Observations retrieved 2026-08-22 from the accounts catalogued in `SUCCESS_ACCOUNTS_RESEARCH.md` (sibling Greenway + two external dealers), mapped onto the pilller/cadence structure already in `CONTENT_STRATEGY_FRAMEWORK.md` and `audit/BENCHMARKS.md`.

---

## 0. Two rules that govern every model

1. **Format yes, hype no.** The observed sibling models are commercial and often hype-toned ("BIGGEST Offer", "Everyone's Talking About"). We adapt the *format, template, and cadence* — we do **not** copy manufactured urgency, which violates CONTENT_STRATEGY_FRAMEWORK §4 (honest-first, no fake urgency, no "act fast" unless the deadline is real).
2. **Nothing unverifiable.** No offer amount, price, APR, incentive, or vehicle that is not confirmed real for the store is ever posted. Every offer/vehicle in these models is **TO VERIFY — request from client via Shannon McNeil** until the store's real inventory/offer feed is provided.

Evidence labels: **OBSERVED** = I saw the exemplar's content on YouTube on 2026-08-22. **TO VERIFY** = needs confirmation before client-facing use.

## Model A — Offer & Incentive Promo

**What it is.** Short, frequent, offer-led items ($/incentive/0%-APR/trade-in bonus/payment hook, seasonal sales), one hook per piece. Drives leads and urgency — used honestly only.

**Exemplars (OBSERVED, 2026-08-22):** Greenway Kia West (e.g., "This Week Only: Extra $1,000 for your trade", "0% APR & No Payments Until Summer", seasonal Black Friday/Christmas), Greenway Kia East ("Model Year End Blowout", "$10,000 over KBB"), Galpin Ford (Memorial Day / Employee Pricing). West also adds a **local-sports hook** ("Drive & Score with Orlando Magic").

**Channel mapping:**
| Channel | Use | Cadence (from framework/BENCHMARKS) |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok | offer Short (≤60s, sound-on captions) | 1–2 of the 3–5 weekly videos |
| YouTube | 1 offer Short/week | fits the 1 Short/week |
| Instagram | offer Reel + Stories | part of 3–5 Reels/Stories weekly |
| Facebook | offer photo/event post | part of 3–5 posts/week |
| GBP | brief offer post + photo | 1 of the 2–4 posts/month |

**Pillars served:** Inventory (offer-on-model) and Community/Local only when the hook is a real local tie.

**Per-store notes:**
- Kia stores (01–04): vehicle offers and seasonal events work; pair every offer with the **real** model/trim on the lot (TO VERIFY inventory feed).
- Store 05 (Ford): same rhythm; Ford-specific promos (Employee Pricing style) only if real.
- **Guardrail:** run this model to ~30% of mix (Inventory default), kept truthful; never advertise an incentive the store cannot honor. Local-sports/community hooks only where the store has a real local tie (TO VERIFY per market).

**Evidence:** OBSERVED (West, East, Galpin). Offer amounts/specifics = TO VERIFY with the store.

## Model B — Per-Vehicle Inventory Walkaround Line (assembly line)

**What it is.** One short video per specific vehicle actually on the lot, coded by stock/VIN, with a consistent template. Proves the lot is real and gives every car a discoverable asset. Scales with inventory, needs no strategy per video.

**Exemplars (OBSERVED, 2026-08-22):** Greenway Ford (`@greenwayford` — "2025 Ford F-150 Lightning SWG10509", "2025 Porsche Cayenne E-Hybrid Coupe", "2026 Ford Bronco Sport…", covering Ford + multiple used brands); Longo Toyota ("2021 Sienna Vehicle Walkaround"). West's 1-minute trim reviews are the new-car cousin of this model.

**Channel mapping:**
| Channel | Use | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube | per-vehicle Shorts | 1 Short/week (house floor) |
| Instagram | inventory Reel/carousel | part of 3–5 weekly Reels |
| TikTok | per-vehicle Short | part of 3–5 weekly videos |
| Facebook | vehicle photo post | part of 3–5 weekly posts |

**Pillars served:** Inventory (dominant). Each vehicle's details = **TO VERIFY** (Inventory pillar rule — never post a vehicle not verifiably on the lot).

**Per-store notes:**
- Store 05 (Ford, pre-owned-heavy market): highest fit — used-lot walkthroughs by VIN, multi-brand.
- Kia stores (01–04): new-arrival walkarounds + used-lot shorts; prioritize the models each market actually buys (TO VERIFY per store).
- Runs the Inventory pillar at or above its 30% default where leads are the goal.

**Evidence:** OBSERVED (Greenway Ford, Longo, West).

## Model C — Model Review / Walkaround Depth

**What it is.** Model- and trim-specific review, comparison, and "why buy this car" content in two lengths: short clips and long-form (7–14 min) tests. Positions the store as an expert and — observed — earns far higher per-video views than offer promos.

**Exemplars (OBSERVED, 2026-08-22):** Greenway Kia North (`@GreenwaykiaNorthFl` — "2027 Kia Telluride X-Pro Off-Road Test" 3.3K views, "Range Rover Just Met Their Competition…" 6.1K views — long-form); Greenway Kia West (1-min trim reviews: Sportage, Sorento, EV6, K4); Greenway Kia East ("Why buy the Kia Optima from…").

**Channel mapping:**
| Channel | Use | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube | long-form monthly + 1 review Short/week | long-form 1/mo; Shorts weekly (framework §6.4) |
| Instagram/TikTok | 30–60s review clips cut from the long-form | part of weekly Reels/videos |

**Pillars served:** Inventory (educational) and Service-adjacent (maintenance/feature explainers).

**Per-store notes:**
- Kia stores (01–04): trim reviews of local best-sellers (Telluride, Sportage, Sorento, Carnival, EV). 
- Store 05 (Ford): F-150 / Bronco / Maverick reviews; comparison "vs the competition" fits trucks.
- **Caution (OBSERVED on North):** content that hypes a "2027 Telluride" before it is deliverable is exactly the kind of forward-model claim we **do not** reproduce. Review only vehicles the store can actually sell, and verify trim/features via the inventory feed.

**Evidence:** OBSERVED (North, West, East). Specific vehicle claims = TO VERIFY.

## Model D — Department, Service & Trust

**What it is.** Spotlight the parts/service bays, finance, mobile service, customer relations, and real customer testimonials. Builds trust, drives service appointments and reviews.

**Exemplars (OBSERVED, 2026-08-22):** Greenway Ford (search-indexed department titles: "Customer Relations", "Mobile Service at Greenway Ford!", "Parts Department", "Finance Department", "Internet Sales Director and Customer Testimonial Video Highlights", "Excellence is the Standard!"); Longo Toyota ("Online Parts Pick Up Made Easy!", "Toyota Certified to Go!").

**Channel mapping:**
| Channel | Use | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook | service reminders, department features, testimonials | part of 3–5 posts/week (Service 20%) |
| YouTube | service explainers/testimonial videos | 1 long-form slot per month if capacity allows |
| GBP | service offer post, photos, review responses | 2–4 posts/mo + 100% review & Q&A response (framework §6.6) |
| Instagram | service-bay Reel/Story | part of weekly Reels/Stories |

**Pillars served:** Service and Reviews & Proof.

**Per-store notes:**
- Store 05 (Ford, service-heavy): highest fit; run mobile-service and parts convenience content.
- Kia stores (01–04): recall/maintenance reminders, "what we check at a service visit" explainers.
- **Reputation-repair stores:** frame as an **absence** problem (OPERATING_RULES §10): "you are not present in the conversation" — never a complaint list.
- Review velocity target is evidence-based (BENCHMARKS §3.2: 20+ recent, 4.0+, monthly new, respond 100% within 2 business days).

**Evidence:** OBSERVED (Greenway Ford, Longo). Store-specific service facts = TO VERIFY.

## Model E — Local/Community & People

**What it is.** Tie the store to its local market: real local partnerships/sports, local events, native-language content where the market warrants it, and the people/place (staff, showroom, delivery day).

**Exemplars (OBSERVED, 2026-08-22):** Greenway Kia West (Orlando Magic "Drive & Score" promotion; Spanish-language Christmas and Black Friday Shorts — 875 views, among the highest on the page); Galpin Ford (showroom walkthrough, supercar halo, personality).

**Channel mapping:**
| Channel | Use | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook | the local hub: events, sponsorships, community posts | part of 3–5 posts/week (Community 20%) |
| Instagram | Stories for timely/local moments + BTS Reels | part of weekly Stories/Reels |
| TikTok | BTS/personality Shorts | part of 3–5 weekly videos |
| YouTube | showroom/personality long-form or Short | as capacity allows |
| Reddit | participation only, after subreddit fit confirmed (framework §6.5) | 2–4 participations/wk after fit |

**Pillars served:** Community/Local, Behind-the-Scenes, Reviews & Proof.

**Per-store notes:** The most market-specific model — West Palm Beach, Jacksonville, Nashville, Antioch, and Raytown each have different local hooks, sports ties, and demographics (**TO VERIFY per store via Shannon/GMs**). Native-language content only where the market genuinely has a large non-English audience (TO VERIFY per market — do not assume). Staff/people content uses **real, named** staff only (names TO VERIFY, OPERATING_RULES §3.5 two-source rule before names enter client-facing copy).

**Evidence:** OBSERVED (West local-sports + bilingual; Galpin showroom). All local hooks and language split = TO VERIFY per market.

## Model selection guidance (per-store)

| If the store's audit/goal is… | Lead with |
|---|---|
| Leads / turnover focus | Model B (inventory line) + Model A (honest offers) |
| Service appointments / trust gap | Model D (service/trust) + Model E (people) |
| Reputation repair (absence problem) | Model D + Model E + Reviews & Proof (BENCHMARKS review velocity) |
| Brand awareness / view growth | Model C (depth reviews earn the most views) balanced with B |
| Local-market standing | Model E (community/people) |

The final per-store mix still comes from `CONTENT_STRATEGY_FRAMEWORK.md` §7 defaults (Inventory 30 / Service 20 / Community 20 / BTS 15 / Reviews 15) — these models are the *formats* that fill those pillars, not a replacement for the mix.

# ADDENDUM (2026-09-03) — Models F–H from the national rooftop comp set

**Source:** `DEALERSHIP_CHANNEL_RANKINGS.md` Part B (franchise volume rooftops, data retrieved 2026-09-03). These three models are the proven external blueprints for the framework's **Tier A / P3 growth engine** — the layer Models A–E did not cover with an at-scale rooftop exemplar. Same two governing rules as above (format yes / hype no; nothing unverifiable).

**Scope note (operator directive, 2026-08-22):** Hyundai stores are out of scope as named benchmarks in anything client-facing. Bakersfield Hyundai observations therefore inform format design internally but are NOT cited to GMs; the client-facing exemplars are Camino Real, Mohawk, Bert Ogden (Kia), and Long McArthur.

## Model F — Daily staff-bit engine (Camino Real Chevrolet blueprint)

**What it is.** One light, people-led short per day: staff humor, family-store moments, quick trends, occasional inventory cameo. No scripts, no set — phone + personality + a daily slot. Camino Real: 161.8K TikTok / 143.4K IG / 5.5M likes, ~7.6% like-to-view on recent posts, built by a family-owned Chevy volume store.
**Feeds:** P3 (TikTok 40% slot), Tier A when a recurring named person carries it, Tier B otherwise.
**Fits:** Store 02 (2–3 willing staff, TO VERIFY names/consent); Store 01 (dedicated video person). Min-viable stores can run it at 2–3/week, not daily.

## Model G — Serialized character mini-series (Mohawk Chevrolet blueprint)

**What it is.** A named, scripted, recurring series with characters and episode structure ("The Dealership": workplace comedy at a Chevy store, ~157K TikTok followers from one creative franchise). Highest ceiling, highest production lift; audience accrues to the SERIES, so consistency is the whole game.
**Feeds:** Tier A hero line, P3.
**Fits:** Store 01 only (the one FULL-stance store with dedicated on-site video capacity). Phase in after 4+ weeks of Model F reps, not before.
**Caution:** Mohawk's IG is 9.2K against 157K TikTok — a series audience does not migrate channels. Judge it on its home channel only.

## Model H — Owner-utility programming (Long McArthur Ford blueprint)

**What it is.** Become the market's useful source for one brand: model news, ordering/config explainers, deep walkarounds, recurring live Q&A. Long McArthur (Salina KS): 76.6K YT subs, ~326K monthly views, weekly live show; the utility line demonstrably sold vehicles nationally.
**Feeds:** P1b depth reviews + P2 deal & ownership education; YouTube-led with FB distribution.
**Fits:** **Store 05 (Ford of KC) almost exactly** — its ratified stance is FB+YT only, Tier-B strong: this is that stance executed at the national-best level, and it beats Bob Sight on watchability without touching IG/TikTok. Kia analog for Stores 01–04: best-seller depth reviews (already the Store 02 wedge).

## Kia-market reference — Bert Ogden "Mission Kia" (Mission, TX)

Largest Kia rooftop found on TikTok (76K followers / 914K likes), bilingual Spanish/English market. Closest single-brand analog for the pilot's Kia stores; pairs with the observed sibling Spanish-language findings (SUCCESS_ACCOUNTS_RESEARCH §1.1). **Content teardown recommended before Store 02/03 calendar revisions.** Language-mix decisions per store remain TO VERIFY.

## Benchmark inputs for `audit/BENCHMARKS.md` / GM expectation-setting

- Best-in-country volume-rooftop outcomes (multi-year): TikTok 130–160K followers · IG 40–145K · YT 15–75K subs · FB 35–125K likes. Six-month pilot targets = a small fraction; report **engagement-per-post**, not follower counts.
- Facebook at even the best rooftops runs sub-1% active engagement → confirms FB's maintenance/community role in the ratified mix (P4-heavy, 5% P3). Do not set FB growth KPIs.
- Zero comp-set winners lead with price/APR content → external validation of the "offer = video with a person, or not posted" rule and the anti-promo-race wedge.
