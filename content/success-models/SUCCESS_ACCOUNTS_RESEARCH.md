## 1. Sibling Greenway Auto Group accounts (benchmarks, not pilot rooftops)

These are useful because they are the same group, same brands, same content suppliers as the pilot — what works (and what to avoid) here transfers most directly. None of these five are pilot rooftops.

### 1.1 Greenway Kia West — YouTube `@GreenwayKiaWest`
- **Status:** CONFIRMED (retrieved 2026-08-22, browser-rendered channel page).
- **Channel:** "Greenway Kia West" · 368 subscribers · 108,025 total views · 6,039 videos · joined Nov 2014. Description: "Welcome to the official Greenway Kia West YouTube channel. We are changing the automotive landscape." Site `greenwaykiawest.com`. Titles repeatedly reference "Greenway Kia West Orlando" → an Orlando-metro rooftop (address TO VERIFY).
- **Channels active:** YouTube confirmed via observation. Facebook/Instagram/TikTok/Reddit/GBP — manual verification needed — 2026-08-22.
- **Observed content types (most recent ~25 videos):**
  1. **Offer / incentive Shorts (0:16–0:34):** "This Week Only: Get an EXTRA $1,000 For Your Trade!" (21 views, 1 mo ago); "Greenway Kia All-In Price | 0% APR & No Payments Until Summer" (132); "$10,000 Over KBB ... Big League Trade-In Event" (19); "How to Get a 2025 Kia K4 for Only $119 a Month?" (39); "Kia Sportage: The Ultimate Lease Hack" (151); "Drive Home a 2025 Kia K4 for Just $159/Month!"; seasonal — "Black Friday Deals at Greenway Kia West 2023" (401), "Freedom Sale", "¡Ofertas del Black Friday!".
  2. **Model-specific review/walkaround Shorts (~1:01):** "2025 Kia K4 GT-Line Turbo Review | Performance, Features & Pricing"; "Kia Sportage Hybrid"; "2025 Kia EV6 Light Long Range Review"; "2025 Kia Sorento S Review"; "2025 Kia Sportage SX-Prestige | Detailed Walkaround"; "2025 Kia Sportage LX Review"; also a "2021 Chevrolet Corvette Stingray Review" (cross-brand variety).
  3. **Local/community tie:** "Kia Drive & Score Promotion with Orlando Magic | Test Drive and Score Free Tickets!" — a real local NBA-team partnership hook.
  4. **Spanish-language content:** "¡Celebra la Navidad con Ofertas Especiales en Greenway Kia!" (875 views — among the highest on the page), "Ofertas del Black Friday" (82) — bilingual content aimed at the Orlando Hispanic market.
- **Observed posting pattern:** very high volume, offer-led, Shorts-first, consistent over years (joined 2014). Views per video are modest (mostly 13–151; a few hundred to ~1.1K on seasonal/Christmas) — **note: engagement is low despite heavy posting**.
- **What makes it effective / what to take:** sustained consistency; clean format templates (one offer per Short, one trim review per Short); local-sports hook; native-language reach. **Caution for our voice:** many titles use manufactured urgency and hype ("Everyone's Talking About", "Can't Ignore", "BIGGEST Offer", "Only While Stock Lasts") that violate our tone guardrails (CONTENT_STRATEGY_FRAMEWORK §4 — no fake urgency). We steal the **format and cadence**, not the **hype tone**.

## 5. Honest bottom line

The strongest **observed** sibling patterns are offer/incentive-heavy (West, East) and per-vehicle inventory assembly lines (Greenway Ford), with depth-content outliers (North long-form) earning the highest per-video views. These are genuine, observed models — but they are **commercial and often hype-toned**, which partly conflicts with our voice guardrails (honest-first, no fake urgency). The reusable value is the **format and cadence discipline**, not the hype copy. Everything here is INTERNAL until Shannon McNeil approves; nothing is client-facing.
