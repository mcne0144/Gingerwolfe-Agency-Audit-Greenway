## 3. What works — ranked, with evidence

1. **Short feature-demo video with a question hook (6–46s).** North: 7.0M / 730K / 218K / 72K / 48K plays. Captions that ask ("Would you use this feature?", "Can your SUV do this?") drive shares (1,500–4,500) and saves (600–16,000), which drive distribution. Needs one phone, one car, one feature. The most repeatable high-ceiling format observed.
2. **Named recurring character with a storyline.** West/Zarya: 1.2M skit, 143.9K serialized bonus arc, and the halo lifts everything she appears in (team intro 2.4K vs 400–700 baseline). Comments concentrate here (934–2,693), i.e., actual conversation.
3. **Offer as character-led video (compliance included).** 118K plays with the full disclaimer in the caption. Offers are not the problem; offer *statics* are.
4. **Launch/new-model walkaround.** West's pinned Seltos launch: 13.5K plays on a 571-follower account (a ~24× follower multiple). K4 Hatch 3.3K. Matches the brief's Tier A "launch walkaround" slot.
5. **Benefits-listicle Reel (✅ checklist).** East Sorento: 4.2K IG + 6.4K/86 FB. No talent needed; works on Facebook, which almost nothing else does.
6. **Utility/service explainers (technician on camera).** Fuel-saving tips 3.9K IG / 2.5K TT; "don't run it on E" 829; alignment 923. Mid-tier but steady, and it is the P2 Service & Ownership engine.
7. **Team/people moments (intro of a real bilingual hire).** 3.7K IG / 2.4K TT with warm comments. People content works when it is specific (a real new person, a real name) rather than generic "meet our team".
8. **Local community sponsorship video.** The one observed (wrestling) was TikTok-weak (872) but the **FB share leader (14 shares)**. Community content is Facebook's format.
9. **Spanish-language line (Orlando).** Consistent mid-hundreds-to-1.7K at East/West. Market-specific evidence; do not port to a pilot market without verifying its language split (`PILLARS.md` §5.1.5).

**What does NOT work (equally important):**
- **Plain offer-copy Reels:** 227–675 plays everywhere, near-zero interaction. The siblings still post 2–4 per month each; the pilot should not.
- **Static graphics, corporate sales-event posts, link posts:** 0–3 reactions, systematically.
- **Delivery-day congrats carousels/slideshows:** 0–15 likes, 500–660 plays, zero shares. Validates the pilot's decision to cut P5b's share and require video-with-story for proof content.
- **Long talking-head trust monologues (2 min):** 804 plays. Trust is shown (demos, transparency math), not told.
- **Letting a channel die:** North's IG has been dormant 27 months while its TikTok took off; the store's best content simply never reaches the IG Reels audience. Cross-posting is nearly free (all three stores already produce vertical video); dormancy is pure account hygiene.

Reading the ratios:
- **Likes ≈ 2–4% of plays** on healthy video, on both TikTok and Reels.
- **Comments are scarce** (0–5) except on character/humor (11–169) and viral posts (302–2,693). Comment count is a character-pillar KPI, not an inventory-pillar KPI. This matches the brief: character wins distribution, education wins intent; do not judge P3 on leads or P1b on comments.
- **Shares and saves are the distribution signal.** Every observed outlier has an abnormal share or save count. Saves flag utility (feature demos, offer math); shares flag humor and community. Sprout should track both explicitly.
- **Follower counts are a lagging vanity floor.** West reaches 2K–13K plays on 571 IG followers; East 4–7K on 426. Reels/TikTok distribution is format-driven. The pilot stores' small followings (TO VERIFY exact counts) are not a ceiling and should not be presented to GMs as one.
- **Facebook is a mirror surface, not a growth surface,** except for community content (shares) and listicles. This supports the ratified FB mix leaning on Community 25% + Reviews/Proof 15% rather than chasing FB virality.

## 5. Cadence that works

- **Active siblings hold ~8–12 posts/month/platform (2–3 per week), weekday, late morning–afternoon ET.** North TikTok: 11 in Aug. West IG: 12 in Aug. East IG: 10 in Aug. This is a proven, sustainable dealership rhythm inside the same group with the same kind of staffing, and it lands inside the house cadence bands in `CONTENT_STRATEGY_FRAMEWORK.md` §8.
- **Mix within that rhythm (observed at the active accounts):** roughly 60–70% video Reels/TikToks, 15–20% offer/promo (which underperforms; the pilot reallocates this), 10–15% statics/carousels (near-zero return; pilot reallocates to Tier C surfaces), and pinned slots reserved for the three best-ever posts.
- **Pinning is load-bearing.** All three of North's and West's pinned TikToks are their all-time top posts; pins keep converting profile visitors. Pilot rule: refresh pins whenever a post outperforms the current pinned set.
- **Consistency beats bursts.** The dormant North IG (zero posts) and the daily-ish East FB feed bracket the range; the engagement winners all sit at steady 2–3/week, not at either extreme.
