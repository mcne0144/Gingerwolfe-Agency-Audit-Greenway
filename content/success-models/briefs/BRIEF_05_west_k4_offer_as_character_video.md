## 2. Why it worked

1. The offer is delivered by a person the audience already knows. Same store, same kind of offer as its 227–436-play plain offer Reels, but with Zarya on camera it did 118K. The analysis doc calls this the cleanest A/B in the dataset, and the frames confirm the only production difference is the character and the structure.
2. It is structured like a tour, not an ad: person, car exterior, interior, walk, store. The offer rides inside content that would hold attention anyway.
3. Compliance strengthens it instead of killing it: the caption carries the full legal disclaimer, so the video itself stays human and unlettered. 1,502 saves means people kept the offer to act on it; saves are the intent KPI here.
4. The only overlay in the whole video is the phone number at the end, a single unambiguous CTA at the moment of highest intent.
5. Low comment count (65) is expected and fine: offer content converts and gets saved, character content gets talked to. Judge this format on saves and calls, not comments (analysis doc §4).

## 3. Reshootable template

1. Prerequisite: one real, GM-confirmed offer on one specific in-stock unit (model, trim, stock number, payment, terms, expiry). If any element is unconfirmed the video does not ship. Offer statics are not a fallback; per the hard rule an offer ships as video with a person or not at all.
2. Cast: the store's character where one exists, otherwise the most camera-comfortable salesperson. Lav mic or a quiet lot; audio must be clean.
3. Shot list around one car, 30–40s: (a) 0–5s hook beside the car, needs-based opening question ("Need an affordable car?"), never a price shout; (b) exterior beats with hands on the car; (c) one interior beat sitting in the cabin; (d) walking beat along the car; (e) closing wide with the store sign in frame and the phone number as the only text overlay.
4. Spoken content: the real payment and the honest qualifiers, in plain speech. No hype copy, no urgency screaming; the tone guardrails in `CONTENT_STRATEGY_FRAMEWORK.md` §4 apply to speech as well as text.
5. Caption: one-line hook, then the complete disclaimer: due at signing, all fees, purchase option, mileage, wear, termination, stock number, expiry, and any compensation disclosure the store uses. Copy the caption architecture of the source post exactly; its completeness is part of why the format is safe to scale.
6. Cross-post to FB and IG Reels the same day.
