# DEALERSHIP_CHANNEL_RANKINGS.md — Top 3 Dealership Channels per Network (National)

**Status:** INTERNAL RESEARCH DRAFT — not client-facing.
**Prepared by:** Bright Matter LLC agency operations team (Content Strategist)
**Observation date:** 2026-09-03 (all data retrieved this date unless noted)
**Scope:** National — all US car-dealership brands (rooftops, dealer groups, and national used-car retailers), NOT limited to Greenway. Companion to `SUCCESS_ACCOUNTS_RESEARCH.md` (which covers Greenway siblings + observed YouTube models).
**Question answered:** What is the highest-performing dealership channel — by followers and engagement — on each of Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube?

---

## 0. Method & data sources

- **YouTube:** vidIQ channel index (subscriber, lifetime view/video counts, trailing-30-day views/uploads). Retrieved 2026-09-03.
- **TikTok:** Direct profile scrape via Apify (clockworks/tiktok-scraper): follower count, lifetime likes, and the 6 most recent non-pinned posts per profile (views/likes/comments/shares) to compute a recent-post engagement sample. Retrieved 2026-09-03.
- **Instagram:** Direct profile fetch (vidIQ IG profile tool): followers, post count, verification. Per-post engagement-rate data was NOT retrievable this pass → engagement noted qualitatively, **TO VERIFY** via Sprout/native insights.
- **Facebook:** Meta gates profile scraping; counts come from web-search snippets of the public pages ("likes" / "talking about this"). Directionally reliable, **TO VERIFY** exact figures.
- **Inclusion rule:** dealership-BRANDED accounts (rooftop, group, or national retailer). Personality accounts owned by dealers/salespeople (Chevy Dude, Russ Flips Whips, George J Saliba) are listed as notables, not ranked — their model isn't directly transferable to a store-branded channel.
- Per OPERATING_RULES §3.7: a failed fetch is never treated as evidence an account doesn't exist; nothing below is fabricated.

## 1. Headline finding

**Vanguard Motor Sales (classic/muscle-car dealer, Plymouth, MI) is the #1 dealership channel on ALL FOUR networks** — 5M Instagram, 3.9M TikTok, 2.8M Facebook, 2.07M YouTube — with genuinely strong engagement (recent TikToks average ~50K views with a ~20% like-to-view ratio; Facebook shows ~273K "talking about this" against 2.8M likes). No franchise new-car rooftop and no national retailer (CarMax, Carvana, AutoNation) comes close. The top of every network is dominated by **specialty-inventory dealers running a daily per-vehicle content assembly line**, not by ad-budget brands.

## 6. Cross-network read (what this means for the pilot)

1. **One brand swept all four networks** — and it's a 40-person classic-car dealer, not a Fortune-500 retailer. The moat is a repeatable content engine: every unit of inventory becomes content on every network, daily, for years. Budget did not decide this; operational cadence did.
2. **National retailers (CarMax/Carvana/AutoNation) have big follower counts and weak organic engagement** — CarMax's ~1.5% like-to-view on recent TikToks and ad-inflated YouTube views show bought reach, not community. Follower count alone is a vanity ranking; we should benchmark on engagement-per-post.
3. **Franchise rooftops crack the top ranks only via a differentiated angle:** staff-culture humor (Bakersfield Hyundai), covetable inventory (Lamborghini Newport Beach), or owner-utility programming (Long McArthur's Ford-news franchise). "Post the offer of the week" appears nowhere near the top on any network.
4. **Transferable to Greenway stores:** (a) per-vehicle walkaround assembly line → our Inventory pillar, directly; (b) people/humor formats → Community & People pillar, the realistic TikTok play for a volume rooftop; (c) utility/answer content → the YouTube play (mirrors the Greenway Kia North long-form finding in `SUCCESS_ACCOUNTS_RESEARCH.md` §1.2). None of these require hype copy, so all fit our voice guardrails.
