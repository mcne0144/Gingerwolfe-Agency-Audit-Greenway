## 8. Brand guardrails (check before drafting copy)

- [ ] **No invented facts.** Every vehicle, price, feature, event, and claim is real and verified, or marked TO VERIFY internally (never published unverified).
- [ ] **No fake urgency.** No "act fast," "only N left," or deadline language unless the deadline/limit is real.
- [ ] **Tone:** honest, specific, helpful, local — a knowledgeable neighbor, not an ad (see `CONTENT_STRATEGY_FRAMEWORK.md` §4).
- [ ] **OEM / compliance rules:** confirmed via Shannon McNeil (TO VERIFY — request from client via Shannon McNeil) and followed.
- [ ] **Reddit-only rule:** if this brief is a Reddit item, it participates — no hard-selling, no promotional links, subreddit fit confirmed (`BENCHMARKS.md` §5).
- [ ] **GBP-only rule:** if a review response — genuine and specific, never templated; respond within 2 business days (`BENCHMARKS.md` §3.2).
- [ ] **Photos/video:** real, current, on-brand; no stock-photo filler; if from the store, confirmed available (TO VERIFY — request from client via Shannon McNeil).
