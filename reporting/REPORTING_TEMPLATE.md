## 6. Quarterly variant (use at end of Months 3 and 6)

The quarterly report is the *shortened* version of the same discipline — the GM wants the arc, not the month-by-month detail (that lives in the monthly reports). Structure:

**Quarterly Performance Report — [Store name]**
1. **Cover** — same block as §1 above, period = "Quarter 1 (Months 1–3)".
2. **Executive summary (3–4 sentences)** — the quarter's net movement on the headline scorecard; the one thing that changed most; the one priority for next quarter.
3. **Trend table** — the headline scorecard rows (from KPI framework §5) × columns **Baseline · Month 1 · Month 2 · Month 3 · Change vs baseline**. One table, no per-channel deep dive (point to the monthly reports).
4. **Movement commentary** — 3 bullets: what moved most (with evidence), what didn't move (with evidence or TO VERIFY), what the platform trends say (engagement fell YoY platform-wide — `BENCHMARKS.md` §1.1; read movement in that context).
5. **What's working / what's not** — top 3 each, evidence-carrying bullets as in §4.
6. **Decisions for next quarter** — table: decision (keep / stop / change / start) | what changes exactly | owner | due. Examples: cadence changes, format shifts, channel on hold (e.g., Reddit if no subreddit fit), review-ask rollout.
7. **Benchmark & audit re-check** — confirm `BENCHMARKS.md` sources were re-verified per its §0; confirm whether a re-audit is due (audit cadence is ~90 days — `AUDIT_FRAMEWORK.md` §4, step 4).
8. **Data sources & gaps (quarterly)** — the §7 appendix, consolidated.

*The quarterly variant never aggregates stores — it is still one store's report.*

## 8. Plain-language glossary (no unexplained acronyms)

| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| **KPI** | Key performance indicator — a number we watch to see if something is working |
| **Reach** | How many different accounts saw the content |
| **Impressions** | How many times content was shown (the same person can count several times) |
| **Engagement rate** | Share of people who saw a post and interacted (like, comment, share, save) — interactions ÷ impressions × 100 |
| **Saves** | People bookmarking a post to find it again — a strong sign of interest |
| **Shares** | People forwarding a post to others |
| **DM** | Direct message — a private message to the store's account |
| **GBP** | Google Business Profile — the store's listing on Google Search and Maps |
| **Direction request** | A shopper tapping "Get directions" on the Google listing — intent to visit |
| **Profile view** | Someone opening the store's profile page |
| **Watch time** | Total hours people spent watching the store's YouTube videos |
| **Subscriber** | Someone who follows the store's YouTube channel |
| **Baseline** | The starting numbers captured at the audit — the point everything is compared to |
| **Trend** | The direction a number moves over time — the report always reads movement over months, not one snapshot |
| **TO VERIFY** | A fact or number that must be confirmed by the store (via Shannon McNeil) before it can be used — never guessed |
| **NAP** | Name, Address, Phone — the three facts that must be identical everywhere the store appears online |
| **Q&A** | Questions and answers on the store's Google profile |
