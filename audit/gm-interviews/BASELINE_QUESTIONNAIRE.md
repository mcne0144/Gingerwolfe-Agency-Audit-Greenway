**How to use.** Ask the questions in order, one at a time. Read them as written; they are phrased for a GM to answer in plain language. Write the answers as they are given. Each question shows what it fills: the audit bucket (Bucket 1–6, per `audit/workbook/WORKBOOK.md`) and the `CLIENT.md` §8 checklist item (§8.1 channel data, §8.2 goals and context, §8.3 logistics). ## Part 1 — Accounts and access (Bucket 1 · §8.1)

*Purpose: find out who controls each account today. This is the biggest Month 1 timeline risk, so this part gets the most time.*

1. Which of these does the store have and use today: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Reddit, and Google Business Profile? For each one, is it active, not used anymore, or never set up? → Bucket 1 · §8.1 (active/inactive status)
2. What are the exact account names or web addresses for each one you just listed? I will write them down. → Bucket 1 · §8.1 (handle/URL)
3. Who at the store can log in to each account today? Names, please. → Bucket 1 · §8.1 (ownership)
4. Does an outside vendor or agency manage any of these accounts? Which ones? → Bucket 1 · §8.1 (vendor split; the Month 1 timeline risk)
5. For Facebook and Instagram, who holds the admin access in Meta Business Manager? → Bucket 1 · §8.1 (access/roles)
6. Who owns the Google Business Profile listing, and is it verified? → Bucket 1 · §8.1 (GBP verification and ownership)
7. Is there one person at the store, or at corporate, who keeps the master list of logins? (Corporate IT is Shawn Vink, per the team's records. Confirm.) → Bucket 1 · §8.1 (access, corporate admin)
8. Are any of the accounts abandoned or orphaned, where nobody at the store can log in anymore? → Bucket 1 · §8.1 (ownership gaps)

## Part 2 — Goals and KPIs (Bucket 2 · §8.2)

*Purpose: what the store wants from the pilot, and what it already measures.*

9. What would a successful 6 months look like for this store? Think about leads, phone calls, service appointments, and awareness. → Bucket 2 · §8.2 (store-level goals)
10. Which matters most right now: new-car sales, used-car sales, or service? Where should new customers come from? → Bucket 2 · §8.2 (goals)
11. Does the store track any numbers today for social or Google, like calls from Google, website clicks, or messages? → Bucket 2 · §8.2 (KPIs already tracked)
12. Roughly how many followers does each account have, and when was the last time each one posted? → Bucket 2 · §8.1 (follower count, last post, cadence)
13. What does the store post about today, and what seems to get the most attention? → Bucket 2 · §8.1 (cadence, content baseline)
14. Roughly how many inquiries come from social media or Google each month: calls, direct messages, or form fills? → Bucket 2 · §8.2 (KPI tracking; call/DM-to-sale conversion stays TO VERIFY until the store confirms it tracks it)

## Part 3 — Operations workflow (Bucket 6 · §8.1)

*Purpose: who posts today, with what tool, on what cadence.*

15. Who posts to the store's accounts today? Names or roles. → Bucket 6 · §8.1 (poster)
16. What do they post with: a scheduling tool (which one?), or posting directly in each app? → Bucket 6 · §8.1 (tool)
17. How often does the store post on each channel: daily, a few times a week, weekly, rarely? → Bucket 6 · §8.1 (cadence)
18. Who answers comments and messages, and how quickly? → Bucket 6 (community management)
19. Is there a content plan or calendar, or does posting happen whenever someone has time? → Bucket 6 (workflow maturity)
20. How much time does that person spend on social each week? → Bucket 6 (effort baseline)

## Part 4 — Content (Bucket 2 inputs · §8.2)

*Purpose: what the store has to work with, and what it is allowed to do.*

21. What photos and videos does the store already have: showroom, inventory, deliveries, the service bay, team members? → §8.2 (content assets)
22. Is there a feed of current inventory the store could pull from, like from the website or the dealer system? → §8.2 (content assets)
23. Are any staff comfortable on camera for short videos? → §8.2 (content assets; also feeds Part 7)
24. Are there brand rules from Kia or Ford, or from corporate, about how the store can advertise or post? → §8.2 (brand/compliance rules)

## Part 5 — Reputation (Bucket 3 · §8.2)

*Purpose: who owns review response today. Frame as neutral questions; this is a one-on-one, and reputation stays absence-framed (guide §4).*

25. Who responds to Google reviews today, if anyone? → Bucket 3 · §8.2
26. Which review sites does the store watch: Google, DealerRater, Cars.com, Edmunds, Yelp? → Bucket 3 · §8.2
27. Do you know what shoppers say about the store on places like Reddit, or does nobody track that? → Bucket 3 (absence-framed; never read a complaint list into this question)

## Part 6 — Pilot logistics (§8.3)

*Purpose: dates, routing, contacts, and reporting expectations.*

28. When should the pilot start, and are there timing constraints, like quarter-end or a model year change? → §8.3 (start date)
29. Who should approve posts before they go out: you, a marketing manager, corporate? → §8.3 (approval routing)
30. Who are the points of contact at the store for content, for access, and for approvals? → §8.3 (contacts)
31. What should a monthly report show, and how often do you want to see it? → §8.3 (reporting cadence expectations)
32. Who sets up the tech, like logins and integrations, if we need access granted? Corporate IT via Shawn Vink? → §8.3 (Sprout Social setup, access)

## Part 7 — Train-the-trainer (§6 long-term objective)

*Purpose: who at the store could eventually run social in-house, per the pilot's long-term objective.*

33. If the pilot works, would the store want to run its social media in-house eventually? → §6 (train-the-trainer)
34. Who on the staff might be a good fit to learn it: someone marketing-minded, good with a phone, comfortable on camera? → §6
35. How much time could a trained employee give to social each week? → §6 (effort availability)

## Part 8 — Soft discovery (Bucket 3–5 signals; no pitching)

*Purpose: surface real openings the store cares about. Ask them conversationally. Do not propose, price, or promise anything. Note what you hear under signals; the team lead logs real openings in `expansion-signals.md` and the operator decides what gets raised later.*

36. What is the biggest frustration with the store's online presence right now? → Bucket 3/4 signal · §8.2
37. When someone searches for the store, what do you think they see? Is the store easy to find? → Bucket 4 signal (search presence)
38. Who are the store's main local competitors, the dealers shoppers cross-shop against? → Bucket 5 (competitive set; this fills the per-market competitor row in the workbook)
