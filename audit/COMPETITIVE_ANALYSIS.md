## Cross-store observation (feeds every rooftop's Bucket 5)

**Trend that holds across all three researched markets (KC, Nashville, Jacksonville) — the static-vs-video reach rule (CONFIRMED 2026-08-23):**
- On every competitor account retrieved (Bob Sight, Rob Sight, Kia on Atlantic), **every static feed post landed 0–3 likes; every video/Reel landed 40–1,680 plays** (COMPETITOR_RESEARCH §7.5.3). This is independent confirmation, from accounts unconnected to Greenway, of the format rule the pilot brief derived from Greenway Kia West. **Reach on these dealer accounts is driven by video, not by static offer graphics.**

**Trend — volume/polish is not the same as reach (CONFIRMED 2026-08-22/23):**
- The most prolific, most polished competitor observed (Bob Sight Ford: 1,919 IG posts, 625 TikToks, near-daily FB) delivers the **worst reach efficiency** in its market (~0.12 plays/follower on TikTok; ~192 median IG Reel plays on 672 followers; 3–17 FB likes on 8.3K followers).
- The scrappiest, smallest account (Rob Sight Ford) out-reaches it ~78× per follower on TikTok (9.4 vs 0.12). **Watchability, not volume or polish, wins reach.**

**Trend — old inventory-video lines are the industry default, not an opening (cite: Waveform 2024, via COMPETITOR_RESEARCH §1.5):**
- Dealer YouTube channels are mostly stale (base rate: ~1/3 of organizations had inactive YouTube; ~half of account-holders published nothing in a quarter). Stale dealership video is the norm, so "competitors are stale" alone is not a durable wedge.

**Finding that matters for audit scoring:** where a competitor is present, the confirmed opening is **depth + utility + watchability** set against content nobody engages with — not "competitors are absent."

## Store 02 — Greenway Kia at the Avenues (Jacksonville, FL) · GM Emre Sevinir

**Set provenance:** open-market discovery (2026-08-22) at operator direction — GM has not provided names (`COMPETITOR_SETS.md` §Store 02). Set: **Kia on Atlantic** (ACTIVE), **Kia of Orange Park** (stale), **Kia Jax** (stale), **Family Kia** (under-observed). Group read: three of four appear to share the "Kia Stores of Jacksonville" group (kiajax.com) — ownership structure TO VERIFY.

### Trends
- **Jacksonville has one current competitor — Kia on Atlantic** (ex-Southside Kia) runs a current, consistent 31-second offer/promo line on YouTube + weekly on Facebook, plus a big organic news moment (its relocation/reopening as "Kia on Atlantic," incl. a Jacksonville radio on-air feature) (CONFIRMED 2026-08-22).
- **Kia on Atlantic's engagement is effectively zero.** 0 likes / 0 shares on four of five observed Facebook posts; the one video drew 2 likes on 379 views. Static 0% APR + holiday-hours graphics with hundreds of words of legal disclaimer (CONFIRMED 2026-08-22).
- **On Instagram** Kia on Atlantic is current and roughly weekly at 98 followers, ~450 median Reel plays (4.6 plays/follower — flattered by a tiny base); static posts 0–3 likes (CONFIRMED 2026-08-23).
- Kia of Orange Park and Kia Jax YouTube lines are **4–5 years stale** (late ~2021/2022, incl. six-figure legacy view outliers that are not current reach) (CONFIRMED 2026-08-22).
- Family Kia: presence confirmed, content depth/recency **under-observed — manual verification needed — 2026-08-22**.

### What's working (for the competitor)
- Currency + consistency + a real local news moment (the relocation) put Kia on Atlantic in front of the market. It owns "current offer/sale + local-moment" video in the Jacksonville Kia market, and it is present on every channel (YouTube, FB, IG) even at near-zero engagement.

### What's NOT working (for the competitor)
- **Nobody engages.** Near-zero likes/shares/plays across a metro of ~1M on a 98-follower IG base. Static offer-graphics with long disclaimers draw 0–3 likes.
- **No competitor runs a current per-vehicle inventory line, current depth reviews, or a people/service/local line.** There is a confirmed depth + utility + local southside gap.

### Competitive implication for Store 02 (Bucket 5)
- Do **not** attempt to out-offer Kia on Atlantic. Win on **depth + utility + local southside**, run current: per-vehicle inventory line, Kia best-seller depth reviews (Telluride, Sportage, Sorento, Carnival, EV9/EV6), people/service/auth + proof, and local Avenues/southside community content.
- Address nuance (CONFIRMED 2026-08-22): Kia on Atlantic is at 11750 Abess Blvd (east Jacksonville), **not** on the southside; Store 02 (10564 Philips Hwy) is genuinely southside. Both serve the southside — frame as local ownership, not head-to-head proximity.

### Customer complaints about competitors

Competing dealers' negative reviews, if any, are TO VERIFY via manual review. Frame as absence, never a complaint list.

## Store 05 — Greenway Ford Kansas City (Raytown, MO) · GM Shane Silvey

**Set provenance:** GM-named (interview Aug 18): **Bob Sight Ford**, **Rob Sight Ford**, **Blue Springs Ford**, **Metro Ford** (`COMPETITOR_SETS.md` §Store 05).

### Trends
- **The KC lane is occupied — this is the market where "competitors are stale" is false.**
  - **Bob Sight Ford**: near-daily FB (8.3K followers), every-other-day TikTok (625 videos), 1,919 IG posts (CONFIRMED 2026-08-22/23). Sharper local-geography copy than our own examples (I-470 commutes, US-50 hauling, Jackson County carpools). BUT the **worst reach efficiency observed in this research**: ~0.12 plays/follower on TikTok (median ~265 plays on 2,302 followers); ~192 median IG Reel plays on 672 followers; 3–17 FB likes on 8.3K followers.
  - **Rob Sight Ford**: 156 TikTok followers, 82 videos, current to Jul 2026 — scrappy personality content (dog as "newest member of the sales team," sales team favorite-car bits). **Best per-post reach efficiency in the KC set** (median ~1,470 plays on 156 followers = 9.4 plays/follower, ~78× Bob Sight). Instagram: current through 7 May 2026, then **stale** — state the split precisely, don't call them "active" flat.
  - **Blue Springs Ford**: per-vehicle used-inventory walkthroughs ~13 yrs old (stale), multi-brand lot (Ford + Dodge, Hyundai, Jeep, VW, Chev, Toyota, Mazda).
  - **Metro Ford**: YouTube is effectively a historical TV-ad/promo archive (11–16 yrs old); "best price" promise with no current content.

### What's working (for the competitors)
- Bob Sight: currency, consistency, local-geographic specificity, real-dollar service offers (mobile service, pickup & delivery).
- Rob Sight: **unpolished, human, funny personality content** — it out-reaches the polished volume player 78× per follower.

### What's NOT working (for the competitors)
- Bob Sight: volume + polish for near-zero engagement (reach problem, not currency problem).
- Rob Sight: not consistent — bursty posting, and Instagram gone stale since May 2026.
- Blue Springs/Metro: abandoned/stale video lines.

### Competitive implication for Store 05 (Bucket 5)
- **Competitive on watchability, not currency and NOT by out-posting them.** Lead with P3/watchability; counter Bob Sight's service utility (collision center, mobile service) with the same utility done as **video with a person**, not price claims. Run the used-lot walkaround line as a Tier C search asset on YouTube/GBP, not a social volume line (full move set: `COMPETITIVE_MOVE_PLAN.md` §1). Proof-over-price vs Metro's empty "best price" promise.
- Caveat: Store 05 has **no IG or TikTok accounts yet** (Phase-1 stand-up per operator 2026-08-22), so no IG/TikTok move is executable until stand-up.
