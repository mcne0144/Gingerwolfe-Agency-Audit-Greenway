What works

The wins were not luck. They were formats.

We studied every top post from your three Orlando stores. The subject of a post barely matters. The format decides everything.

On Kia West, a lease offer posted as a static image drew two likes. The same offer, delivered by a salesperson on camera, drew 120,000 plays and 1,500 saves. Same store, same month, same offer.

The weekly system

Under thirty minutes of your time per week.

Everything else is done by us, or by the person on your floor who is already good at this.

  1. We send the brief on Monday

    Which format, who is on camera, three shots, the caption already written.

  2. Your person shoots it on a phone

    One take, native sound, fifteen minutes on the lot.

  3. We cut it for every channel

    One capture becomes the TikTok, the Reel, the Facebook post, and the YouTube Short.

  4. We answer the comments and route the leads

    Anyone showing intent goes to your store, with a copy kept in Sprout.

  5. The scorecard tells us what to make next

    Your own numbers, against your own baseline, not against Orlando.
