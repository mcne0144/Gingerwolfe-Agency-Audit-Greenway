## 2. How this research feeds the content strategy

1. **Feeds per-store strategy.** `COMPETITIVE_MOVE_PLAN.md` maps each move to the five content models in `content/success-models/CONTENT_MODELS.md` (A–E) and the pillars/channels in `content/CONTENT_STRATEGY_FRAMEWORK.md`. When the Content Strategist builds a store's `STRATEGY.md` and calendars, the competitive research is one input into which pillars dominate and how the store differentiates — alongside the store's audit and goals (still TO VERIFY).
2. **Signals the wedge per market.** CONFIRMED 2026-08-22: the named external competitors' YouTube lines in the **KC and Nashville** markets are stale/dormant — point those stores at owning the **current, consistent, real** video lane. **Jacksonville (Store 02) is the exception:** **Kia on Atlantic** runs a current offer/promo line there, so Store 02's wedge is **depth + utility + local** (inventory line, depth reviews, people/service) that no Jacksonville competitor runs — not "competitors are absent."
3. **Maintains the sibling-vs-competitor boundary.** Greenway Kia Franklin is a **sibling benchmark**, not a competitive threat. Its content-pattern insights route to the **sibling success-model set** (`content/success-models/`), never to the "local competitor" threat analysis, and it is never surfaced to a GM as a rival. The classification decision is logged for the operator in `COMPETITOR_SETS.md`.
4. **Enforces the operator's competitor boundary.** Any **Kia+Hyundai combined dealership or Hyundai dealership is OUT OF SCOPE** — not a competitor, not a benchmark, not a gap — per operator directive 2026-08-22. No Hyundai store appears in any competitor set or move plan.
