## 2. What changed from revision 1, and why

Revision 1 was built on one wedge: competitors are stale, so post current content and own the lane.
Platform data collected 2026-08-22 does not support that wedge on the channels the pilot publishes to.

### Finding 1 — the stale-competitor wedge was a YouTube artifact (CONFIRMED)

Revision 1 assessed competitors only on YouTube. On TikTok and Facebook:

| Competitor | TikTok | Facebook | Revision 1 said |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bob Sight Ford | 2,302 followers, **625 videos**, posting every other day | 8.3K followers, near-daily | "thin, a mobile-service slot ~2 years old" |
| Rob Sight Ford | 156 followers, 82 videos, current | — | "inventory line 8 years old" |
| Wyatt Johnson Kia | 30 followers, 2 videos, last post Feb 2025 | — | "near-dormant" (correct) |
| Kia on Atlantic | not found | weekly posts, **0 likes / 0 shares on 4 of 5** | "current, consistent offer line" (correct) |

Bob Sight also publishes local-geography trim comparisons written around I-470 commutes, US-50 hauling
and Jackson County carpools — better local specificity than revision 1's own examples. The Kansas City
lane is not empty. Store 02's wedge, by contrast, is stronger than revision 1 claimed.

Base rate worth knowing: across 2,824 organizations, ~32% had inactive YouTube accounts and nearly half
of account-holders published nothing in a quarter. A stale dealership YouTube channel is the industry
default, not a market opening.

### Finding 2 — what works at Greenway is structural, not topical (CONFIRMED)

Greenway Kia West's results come from three things revision 1 had no concept for:

1. **A named recurring on-camera character.** "Zarya" carries the account's biggest posts.
2. **Serialized campaigns with a stated goal.** "Help Zarya get to 15k followers" ran at least 22 days
   as a numbered series. West sits at 14,000 TikTok followers — built by a campaign, not a content mix.
3. **Launch walkarounds.** The 2027 Seltos full walkaround is their #1 Instagram and #2 TikTok post.

| West's top posts | Plays |
|---|---|
| "Who would you buy a car from?" (character, humour) | **1,200,000** |
| 2027 Seltos full walkaround (92s, launch model) | 216,600 |
| 2027 Seltos second post | 150,500 |
| "Help Zarya get the $5000 bonus" (character campaign) | 143,800 |
| "Need an affordable car?" (offer, as video) | 116,100 |

Revision 1 said "do not clone the Kia Guy," treating the personality format as a stale competitor
artifact. Greenway runs that model successfully in-house right now. What should not be cloned is
Murfreesboro's *person*. The *format* is proven.

It also launches cold accounts: Store 04's **9-follower** account posted a sales-team introduction and
reached 1,384 plays at a 21% like rate.

### Finding 3 — format decides, not topic (CONFIRMED)

| Same topic, different format | Result |
|---|---|
| "Need an affordable car?" — video, full lease disclaimer | **116,100 plays** |
| "The Truth About '$0 Down' Leases" — static image | **2 likes** |
| "August Pre-Owned Sales Event" — static image | **0 likes** |

Across a 29-post Instagram sample: every static image and carousel landed 0–15 likes. Every Reel landed
342–13,055 plays.

### Channel reality (CONFIRMED, Greenway Kia West)

| Channel | Routine post | Ceiling |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | ~2,050 plays | 13,055 |
| TikTok | ~1,030 plays | **1,200,000** |
| Facebook | ~482 views, ~2 likes | 1,237 |

Instagram is the reliable floor. TikTok is the ceiling. Facebook draws 0–5 likes on the same posts that
reach 3,000+ on Instagram — modal like count across 21 posts is zero.

## 4. The format layer (new)

Pillars say what a post is *about*. Two posts in the same pillar can differ 1000x on format alone.
Every planned post carries a tier.

**Tier A — Hero.** Recurring named character, serialized campaign, or launch walkaround. Where the
ceiling lives; all six-figure posts are Tier A. **Target: 1+ per rooftop per week.**

**Tier B — Workhorse.** Video, no character required. Feature demos, service explainers, comparisons,
offer-as-video, delivery-day video. The reliable ~2,000-play Instagram baseline.

**Tier C — Utility. Published but NOT counted.** Per-VIN walkarounds, static offer and compliance
graphics, hours/holiday notices, roundup photos, review-milestone graphics. Real jobs — search assets,
compliance, reference — but not social content. They live on YouTube, GBP and Facebook.

**Two hard rules:**
- Every counted social post is Tier A or Tier B. Tier C never fills a mix slot.
- An offer is published as video with a person in it, or it is not published as a social post.

## 5. The five pillars

Same five as revision 1. Each now has a boundary and an exclusion so posts tag to exactly one pillar.

### Pillar 1 — Inventory: "The Real Cars on Our Lot"
Goal: leads and dealership visits. **Is:** the vehicle is the subject. **Is not:** a staff member as
subject (P3), or a service/ownership explainer (P2).

Split into two products, counted separately:
- **1a — search asset (Tier C).** Per-VIN walkarounds, VIN-coded, batch, on YouTube + GBP. Judged on
  search surfacing and VDP traffic. **Consumes no social mix share.**
- **1b — social content (Tier A/B).** Launch walkarounds, feature demos, comparisons, offer-as-video.

Calendar rule: model-launch cadence drives 1b. The launch walkaround is the only inventory format in the
data that reaches six figures.

### Pillar 2 — Service & Ownership
Goal: service appointments, plus the questions that precede a purchase. **Is:** what we do — process,
department, utility, explainers. **Is not:** what customers say (P5).

Renamed from "Service & Trust" to house the category revision 1 left homeless: **deal and ownership
education** — trade-in, financing, lease-end, first service, what "$0 down" actually means.

Note: at Store 05 this is contested ground. Bob Sight already runs virtual collision estimates, mobile
service and pickup-and-delivery, each with a real dollar offer.

### Pillar 3 — People & Character
Goal: build audience and a recognizable voice. **This is the growth engine.** **Is:** our people at our
store, with a running storyline. **Is not:** our people at a community event (P4).

Re-specified around a **named person with a running storyline**, not staff intros and "a day at the
dealership."

Honest caveat: buyer research finds only 19% of shoppers express interest in behind-the-scenes content.
Not a contradiction — different measures. Character content wins **distribution** (what the algorithm
shows non-followers); education content wins **intent** (what a shopper seeks out). P3 buys the reach
that P1 and P2 convert. Do not measure it on lead volume.

Fragility: West's character posts cluster Jan–Jun; recent months revert to templated product captions at
far lower reach. Character production decays first and needs protecting in the calendar.

### Pillar 4 — Community & Local
Goal: local-market standing. **Is:** the store in its market — partnerships, events, local moments,
native-language content where warranted. **Is not:** staff personality shot on the lot (P3).

**GATED.** Every input is TO VERIFY at all five rooftops. Opens at 10%, rises to 20% only once that
store's hook list is confirmed. Otherwise it fills with weather, holidays and landmark photos — exactly
the generic content the pillar exists to avoid.

### Pillar 5 — Reviews & Proof
Goal: convert near-decision shoppers, feed review velocity. **Is:** what customers say. **Is not:** our
own process (P2).

Split, because the halves behave differently:
- **5a — GBP review operations.** Full genuine response within 2 business days, profile completeness,
  photo refreshes. A named GM KPI and a recurring calendar row — **but not a post share.**
- **5b — social proof content (Tier A/B only).** Delivery-day video with a real person, testimonial
  video. Counted in the mix.

Why the post share drops: happy-customer carousels drew 4–15 likes, review and milestone graphics 0–3.
Proof converts on Google, not in an Instagram carousel.

Blended: roughly **32 / 22 / 22 / 11 / 9** against revision 1's 30 / 20 / 15 / 20 / 15. The moves are

**Two capacity variants.** *Full mix* above, for a store that can supply media and put a named person on
camera weekly. *Minimum viable mix* for a store that cannot: P3 drops to 10%, its share moving to 1b and
2, Tier A met by launch walkarounds alone. A holding pattern, not a destination.

## 8. Measurement and decision rules

### External calibration (Emplifi auto-dealership, Q2 2026; 3,957 FB pages / 981 IG profiles)
- Median engagement rate: Instagram ~3.1%, Facebook ~0.85%.
- Median interactions per post: IG Reels 24, carousels 19; FB Live 15, standard video ~2.5.
- Q4 2025 cross-platform: TikTok ~2x Instagram median interactions, ~20x Facebook. Instagram organic
  reach fell 30–40% across all formats during 2025.

### Internal reference (Greenway, CONFIRMED)
- Kia West: IG routine ~2,050 plays; TikTok routine ~1,030, ceiling 1,200,000.
- Kia North: TikTok routine ~845 plays on 33,900 followers.
- Cold-start proof: 9-follower account, team-intro post, 1,384 plays at 21% like rate.

### Per pillar

| Pillar | Primary metric | Reallocation rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1a Inventory (search) | VDP/site sessions from YouTube + GBP | Cut to maintenance cadence if no lift by day 90 |
| 1b Inventory (social) | Reach and saves vs store median | Shift toward whichever of launch/demo/comparison leads by day 60 |
| 2 Service & Ownership | Service appointment starts from social + GBP | Hold — revenue pillar |
| 3 People & Character | Follower growth; reach per post vs store median | Raise wherever it beats store median for 60 days |
| 4 Community & Local | Local engagement, in-market comments | Cannot exceed 10% until hook list confirmed |
| 5a GBP review ops | New reviews/month; response within 2 business days | Hold — named GM KPI |
| 5b Social proof | Reach vs store median | Drop to 5% if it trails store median for 60 days |

### Kill criteria, pre-committed
1. **Static offer graphics** are not published as counted social posts from day one. Evidence already
   sufficient; no trial needed.
2. **Per-VIN walkarounds** are Tier C from day one, never fill a social slot. Reviewed at day 90 on
   search and VDP lift alone.
3. **Any pillar with no verified inputs by day 30** reverts its share to pillars that have inputs.
4. **Character line:** if a rooftop cannot field a named on-camera person by day 30, Tier A is met by
   launch walkarounds only and P3's share moves to 1b and 2 there. A capacity finding, not a content
   failure.
5. **Any rooftop still running duplicate accounts at day 30** is a blocked baseline, not underperformance.

## 10. Verification checklist (TO VERIFY — request via Shannon McNeil)

- Canonical handle and access per rooftop per channel, duplicates resolved. **Publishing cannot start
  until this is done.**
- Real inventory feed: models, trims, stock/VIN, new vs used. Never post a car not verifiably on the lot.

- Store goals, assets, content capacity — determines full vs minimum viable mix.
- OEM rules: Kia (01–04) and Ford (05) advertising, co-op, vehicle-claim and incentive rules. Verify each
