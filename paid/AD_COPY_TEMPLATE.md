## 1. How to use this template

This is a **structure-only template**. It holds the skeleton of a GM-ready ad: fields, character limits, asset specs, and the placeholders to fill. It contains **no invented offers, no vehicle claims, no prices, no inventory**. Every store-specific fact carries a placeholder or a **TO VERIFY — request from client via Shannon McNeil** marker.

Numbered workflow for one ad:

1. Pick the ad slot from the store's content calendar (`content/calendars/store-XX/`) — paid ads amplify approved organic content, they do not invent new claims.
2. Fill the Creative Brief (§3) for that ad: objective, audience, format, asset, hook.
3. Fill the platform copy blocks (§4) for the target platform(s).
4. Every factual field (vehicle, price, offer, hours, phone, address) is either verified or **TO VERIFY — request from client via Shannon McNeil**. Nothing published with a placeholder.
5. Submit to team lead → Shannon McNeil approval → **BLOCKED until operator confirms scope expansion + access** → only then does a launch checklist (`CAMPAIGN_SETUP_GUIDE.md`) apply.

**Voice rules (from `CONTENT_STRATEGY_FRAMEWORK.md` §4):** honest first (no fake urgency, no "only 2 left" unless the limit is real), specific beats vague (real model, real trim, real location), helpful beats pushy, human not corporate, local first. Plain language a GM and a shopper both understand.

## 4. Per-platform ad copy blocks

### 4.1 Meta — Facebook + Instagram

**Specs (verify in-platform at build — Meta changes these; canonical doc per `BENCHMARKS.md` §8):**
- Primary text: up to ~125 characters before "See more" in feed (full text allowed; keep the first 2 lines the hook). No hard platform cap on full text; house rule: ≤125 chars for the visible hook, rest supporting.
- Headline: ≤40 characters (house rule; platform max is higher).
- Description: ≤30 characters (house rule).
- CTA button: choose from platform list (Call Now, Get Directions, Send Message, Learn More, Book Now, etc.).
- Media: photo 1080×1080 (square) or 1080×1350 (portrait); video 1080×1920 (Reels) or 4:5; keep the top/bottom thirds clear of text on Reels (house rule).

**Copy skeleton (fill placeholders; nothing invented):**

```
Primary text:
[HOOK — one line, local + specific, e.g., "New Kias are landing at [STORE] this week."]
[PROOF — one line, real, e.g., "4.6★ from our Jacksonville neighbors." — TO VERIFY]
[DETAIL — one line, e.g., "Stop by [ADDRESS] or call [PHONE]." — TO VERIFY]
[OFFER — ONLY if verified: e.g., "[OFFER]" — TO VERIFY]

Headline: [e.g., "[MODEL] at [STORE]" — TO VERIFY]
Description: [e.g., "[CITY/MARKET]'s Kia dealer since [YEAR]" — TO VERIFY]
CTA button: [Call Now / Get Directions / Send Message / Learn More / Book Now]
```

**Hook bank (Meta):**
- H1 Local new-arrival: "New [MODEL]s are on the lot at [STORE]." (inventory TO VERIFY)
- H2 Service reminder: "Is it time for [SERVICE-TIP]? Ask the service team at [STORE]." (tip TO VERIFY)
- H3 Proof: "See why [CITY/MARKET] rates [STORE] [RATING]★." (rating TO VERIFY)
- H4 Community: "We're your neighbors on [STREET]. Come say hi." (address TO VERIFY)
- H5 Question hook (engagement): "What's your first question when buying a car? Ask us." (no claims)

### 4.2 TikTok

**Specs (official specs page 403'd from this environment — manual verification needed — 2026-08-12; use these house defaults and confirm in Ads Manager at build):**
- Video: vertical 9:16, 1080×1920; duration 5–60s (house default 15–30s for tests).
- On-screen text/captions: required (sound-on behavior — `CONTENT_STRATEGY_FRAMEWORK.md` §6.3).
- Spark Ads: uses the store's OWN organic video (author handle + existing engagement preserved) — the default for the pilot's first tests (`PAID_MEDIA_FRAMEWORK.md` §6).
- CTA: TikTok native (Visit website / Send message / Get directions / Lead form).

**Copy skeleton (for the ad text + on-screen captions):**

```
Caption (ad text, ≤~100 chars visible before truncation; full allowed):
[HOOK — 3–6 words, e.g., "POV: you found your next Kia."]
[ONE line of real detail, e.g., "[MODEL] walkaround at [STORE]" — TO VERIFY]
[CTA, e.g., "Tap for [OFFER/directions/lead form]" — OFFER TO VERIFY]

On-screen text (3 beats max):
Beat 1 (0–2s): [HOOK, e.g., "New [MODEL] in the showroom"]
Beat 2 (mid): [real detail, e.g., "[SERVICE-TIP] / walkaround start"]
Beat 3 (end): [CTA, e.g., "Call [PHONE]" — TO VERIFY]

CTA: [Visit website / Send message / Get directions / Lead form]
```

**Hook bank (TikTok):**
- T1 "POV" hook: "POV: you're doing a walkaround of the new [MODEL] at [STORE]." (model TO VERIFY)
- T2 Question hook: "What's the one thing to check before you buy a used car?" (tip TO VERIFY — helpful beats pushy)
- T3 Day-in-the-life: "A morning at [STORE]: [real scene, e.g., detail bay, delivery day]." (scene TO VERIFY)
- T4 Trend-fit: [only trends that fit a dealership — house rule; never forced — `CONTENT_STRATEGY_FRAMEWORK.md` §6.3]
- T5 Service myth-buster: "[SERVICE-TIP myth] — real answer from our service team." (tip TO VERIFY)

### 4.3 Reddit

**Specs (Reddit for Business, fetched live 2026-08-12 — ad types: Image, Video, Carousel, Conversations; re-verify at build):**
- Reddit ads run from a Reddit ads/business account (separate from any organic u/ account — structure TO VERIFY with Shannon).
- Ad types for dealership entry: **Conversations Ad** (post-style ad with discussion) or carousel/image targeted by community + keyword.
- No hard character cap stated here (Reddit supports long-form by nature); house rule: title ≤ 90 chars, body as short as a good Reddit post — 2–4 sentences.
- **Voice warning (hard rule):** Reddit's Reddiquette — far more participating than promoting (`BENCHMARKS.md` §5). The ad must read like a useful local answer, not a billboard. No hype words, no fake urgency, no "ACT NOW." Ads that read like ads get ratio'd in the comments.

**Copy skeleton (Conversations-style):**

```
Title (≤90 chars):
[USEFUL QUESTION/STATEMENT, local + specific, e.g., "What do [CITY/MARKET] drivers ask before
buying a Kia? We asked our sales team." — no offer in the title]

Body (2–4 sentences):
[REAL answer/tip — TO VERIFY with store team, e.g., "[SERVICE-TIP]"]
[One honest line about the store, e.g., "We're [STORE] on [ADDRESS] — here when you need us." — TO VERIFY]
[No price, no urgency, no superlatives]

Call to action (choose one, keep soft):
"Happy to answer questions here." / "Come see us on [STREET]." / "Ask us anything in the comments."
```

**Hook bank (Reddit):**
- R1 Answer-first: "Here's how [common local question] works in [CITY/MARKET]." (answer TO VERIFY)
- R2 Myth-busting: "A common myth about [car topic] — what our service team actually sees." (TO VERIFY)
- R3 Honest-local: "What should you know before visiting a dealership in [CITY/MARKET]?" (no claims)
- R4 Never: "BEST DEALS!!", price lists, "only X left", anything that breaks Reddiquette.

### 4.4 YouTube / Google

**Specs (YouTube/Google Ads — confirm in-platform at build):**

- CTA: platform buttons (Call, Visit site, Get directions).
- **Existing-account rule (hard line):** if any Greenway Google Ads account exists, no agent touches it. New campaigns only in a NEW account structure created under expanded scope + access (`PAID_MEDIA_FRAMEWORK.md` §8).

**Copy skeleton:**

```
Headlines (up to 3, each ≤30 chars — house rule):
1. [e.g., "[MODEL] at [STORE]" — TO VERIFY]
2. [e.g., "[CITY/MARKET] Kia dealer" — TO VERIFY]
3. [e.g., "Service at [STORE]" — TO VERIFY]

Descriptions (up to 2):
1. [e.g., "[MODEL] walkarounds + real inventory at [STORE]." — TO VERIFY]
2. [e.g., "Call [PHONE] or stop by [ADDRESS]." — TO VERIFY]

Video script beats (from the store's real footage):
Beat 1 (0–3s): [hook — the car/place/person on screen, with caption]
Beat 2 (3–20s): [real detail — walkaround, tip, or delivery story — TO VERIFY]
Beat 3 (final 5s): [CTA on screen + spoken, e.g., "Call [PHONE] / Get directions"]
```

**Hook bank (YouTube/Google):**
- Y1 Model search-match: "[MODEL] in [CITY/MARKET] — at [STORE]." (model/inventory TO VERIFY)
- Y2 Service query-match: "[SERVICE-TIP] — what [CITY/MARKET] drivers ask." (tip TO VERIFY)
- Y3 Proof: "Real walkaround: [MODEL] at [STORE]." (TO VERIFY)

## 5. Universal guardrails (apply to every platform)

1. **No invented facts, offers, prices, inventory, or performance claims.** Every factual field is verified or **TO VERIFY — request from client via Shannon McNeil**. A placeholder never ships.
2. **No fake urgency or scarcity.** "Act fast," "only N left," "final days" only when the deadline/limit is real (`CONTENT_STRATEGY_FRAMEWORK.md` §4; Acceptable Use).
3. **No hype adjectives** (unbelievable, amazing, crazy deals). Specific beats vague.
4. **Real local proof only** — real lot photos (never stock passed off as the lot), real review quotes with capture date, real staff with names TO VERIFY.
5. **Reddit voice is participation, not broadcast** (`BENCHMARKS.md` §5) — even in paid.
6. **Inventory posts require a verified inventory feed** (`CLIENT.md` §8.2) — never a car that is not verifiably on the lot.
7. **Every ad carries the approval trail** (drafted → team lead → Shannon → gate) and the **BLOCKED** marker until scope expansion + access.
8. **No GM is ever pitched this template's output.** It exists to serve Shannon's expansion conversation with the client.
