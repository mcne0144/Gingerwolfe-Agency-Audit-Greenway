## What this folder is

The paid-media foundation for Greenway Auto Group's six-month organic pilot. It is a **plan-and-draft** package: strategy, ad copy templates, build checklists, and expansion opportunities. **No live ad spend exists, has existed, or will run through this team until Shannon McNeil confirms expanded scope and access.** The signed SOW is organic-only.

> **THE HARD LINE (applies to every file in this folder):**
> No agent launches, funds, modifies, or manages live ad spend, and no agent touches any existing Greenway ad account (including Facebook paid, where Greenway-side contact Brennan has an informal role) without the operator's explicit confirmation that scope has expanded and access has been granted.
> Every step that would touch a live account or spend carries the gate: **"BLOCKED until operator confirms scope expansion + access."**
> The retainer ($10,000/month flat, $2,000/rooftop) is the SERVICE FEE, not media spend. Every budget here is a **PROPOSAL** for Shannon's approval — never presented as real spend, never quoted to a GM as fact.

## How paid relates to the rest of the workspace

```
content/ (organic calendar + creative briefs)
   │  ad creative is BUILT from the store's own approved organic content
   ▼
paid/ (this folder — strategy, copy templates, build guides, opportunities)
   │  budgets and tests are PROPOSALS for Shannon's approval
   ▼
reporting/ (KPI_FRAMEWORK.md, GM_KPI_FRAMEWORK.md, dashboards)
   │  paid KPIs map to existing K-numbers (reach K1, impressions K2,
   │  engagement K3, DMs K9, link taps K12, views K13, calls K19,
   │  directions K18, website clicks K20, reviews K21) — combined view in
   │  PAID_MEDIA_FRAMEWORK.md §10
   ▼
WORKFLOW.md (specialist drafts → team lead reviews → Shannon McNeil approves → client)
   ▼
expansion-signals.md (Paid media openings logged for the operator's review —
                      she decides what gets raised, when, and with which GM)
```

The rules that hold it all together:

1. **Organic content feeds ad creative.** Ads amplify the store's own proven posts (framework §2 Layer 1: only boost top organic performers). No ad invents content the store has not produced, and no inventory post ships without a verified inventory feed (`CLIENT.md` §8.2 — TO VERIFY).
2. **The KPI framework measures results.** Ad KPIs (CTR, CPM, CPC, leads LD-1, GBP actions from ads) are defined in framework §10.1 and mapped to existing K-numbers — they extend the set, never replace it, and need the Reporting Analyst's + Shannon's sign-off on definitions.
3. **Budgets flow to Shannon for approval.** Every budget is a PROPOSAL. The retainer is not media spend. No budget is discussed with a GM before Shannon approves the proposal.
4. **Nothing runs without her sign-off.** The approval pipeline (`WORKFLOW.md` §1) applies to every paid artifact, and the scope-expansion + access gate (`CAMPAIGN_SETUP_GUIDE.md` §1) applies to every live action.
5. **Openings from this analysis go into `expansion-signals.md`.** `PAID_OPPORTUNITY_TEMPLATE.md` §3 holds drafted rows (evidence-backed, observed 2026-08-12) that the operator can review, edit, and raise one-on-one. No agent pitches, proposes, or prices services to anyone at Greenway.
6. **Benchmarks are labeled by source type** (`OPERATING_RULES.md` §3.6): Sprout Social (third-party, verified live 2026-08-12) for TikTok minimums and Spark Ads; Reddit for Business (platform-published marketing) for Reddit reach/communities/ad types; blocked pages (TikTok specs, Reddit budget FAQ) marked **manual verification needed — 2026-08-12**. Nothing invented.
